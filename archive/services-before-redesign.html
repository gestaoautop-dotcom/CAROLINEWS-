MY PROCESS / FROM DIAGNOSIS TO SOLUTION

First, the why.
Then, the solution.

I start by understanding how your business works. Then I connect brand, communication and technology to build a solution with your identity and your way of working.

01 Audit→ 02 Branding or rebranding→ 03 Positioning and communication→ 04 Business needs→ 05 Everything connected
  1. 01

    UNDERSTAND

    Audit

    Before proposing, I investigate.

    I look at the brand, digital presence, communication and operations. I listen, observe and compare what the company wants to convey with what customers actually perceive.

    What I look at +

    Current identity, channels, customer experience, existing tools, team workflows, friction points and opportunities.

    What this step delivers A diagnosis of what works, what needs to change and what should come first.

  2. 02

    BUILD OR REFRESH

    Branding
    or rebranding

    A brand that fits the business.

    With the diagnosis in hand, I build the brand or rethink the existing one. Its identity gains direction, personality and consistency with the company's story and goals.

    What I develop +

    Brand concept, visual identity, colors, typography, guidelines and applications. For rebranding, I assess what should evolve and what is worth keeping.

    What this step delivers A consistent brand foundation to guide the next decisions.

  3. 03

    SET THE DIRECTION

    Positioning
    and communication

    Clarity on why people should choose your brand.

    I define how the brand should be perceived, who it speaks to and how it expresses its value. That direction comes through in words, images and every touchpoint.

    What we align +

    Audience, differentiators, value proposition, tone of voice, key messages, content and communication channels.

    What this step delivers A clear, recognizable message aligned with the brand identity.

  4. 04

    TURN NEEDS INTO FEATURES

    Business needs

    Daily work reveals what needs to be built.

    I dig into the processes: how customers find you, how service happens, how the team works and where information gets lost. I turn those needs into priorities and features.

    What we map +

    Customer journeys, manual tasks, records, quotes, scheduling, management, integrations and automation opportunities, shaped around each company's reality.

    What this step delivers A custom scope with the features your operations actually need.

  5. 05

    CONNECT EVERYTHING

    Brand + positioning
    + custom software.

    What the business is, what it communicates and how it works become part of one experience.

    01 / IDENTITY

    Your brand.

    Your visual identity and personality in every detail.

    02 / DIRECTION

    Your positioning.

    A consistent message across every touchpoint.

    03 / OPERATIONS

    Your software.

    A website, tools and workflows built for your day-to-day operations.

    From how people see your brand to how your business works.

Every project starts with understanding your reality.
